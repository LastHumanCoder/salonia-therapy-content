Online Therapy in California

Warm, evidence-based, culturally informed care for individuals and couples, from wherever you feel most yourself.

Online therapy California residents can trust means meeting a California-licensed therapist over secure, encrypted video, from home, work, or anywhere in the state. At Salonia Singh Therapy and Associates, our online therapy California sessions are the same warm, 50-minute, evidence-based, culturally informed appointments we offer in our West Los Angeles and Pasadena offices, matched to the right clinician for you.

For many people, online therapy California removes the barriers that once made care feel out of reach: long commutes, few local providers, or a schedule with little room to spare. Whether you prefer online therapy California sessions full time or a mix of virtual and in person, all you need is a private space and a stable connection.

Who we help

We work with individuals and couples across California who want secure virtual sessions with a therapist who understands their world. Many of the people we support are high-achieving South Asian, BIPOC, and immigrant professionals.

Individuals

Anxiety, depression, burnout, imposter syndrome, people-pleasing, self-esteem, childhood trauma, and career or identity transitions.

Couples

Communication, conflict, trust, and reconnection, with clinicians trained in the Gottman Method and Emotionally Focused Therapy.

Culturally informed care

Space for family, faith, migration, and bicultural identity, so you do not have to translate your world for your therapist.

How online therapy works with us

You start with a free consultation, we learn a little about what you are navigating, and we match you with a clinician on our team whose training and style fit your needs. From there, sessions run 50 minutes over a secure, HIPAA-appropriate video platform. Because our clinicians are licensed in California, we can see clients located anywhere in the state at the time of the session, from San Diego to the Bay Area to the Central Valley.

Our approach is warm but direct, and always evidence-based. Clinicians draw on methods including EMDR, the Gottman Method, Emotionally Focused Therapy, and Internal Family Systems, adapted thoughtfully to a virtual setting. Research on telehealth in the mental-health field generally finds that online therapy can be as effective as in-person care for many common concerns.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to live in a specific city to use your telehealth?

No. Because our clinicians are licensed in California, we can offer secure online therapy to anyone located anywhere in the state during the session, from Los Angeles and Pasadena to more rural counties. You only need a private space and a stable internet connection.

How long are sessions and how do I start?

Sessions are 50 minutes, the same as our in-person appointments. To begin, book a free consultation through our contact page. We will learn a little about what you are looking for and match you with a clinician whose training and style fit your needs.

Is telehealth private and secure?

Yes. Sessions take place over a secure, encrypted video platform appropriate for health information, and you receive a private link for each appointment. We recommend joining from a quiet, private space where you will not be overheard, so you can speak freely.
