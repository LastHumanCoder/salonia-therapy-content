For accomplished professionals who look successful on the outside and feel never enough on the inside.

Therapy for high achievers is specialized support for driven, self-aware professionals whose outward success can hide anxiety, perfectionism, and self-doubt. Our California practice offers therapy for high achievers that is both evidence-based and culturally informed, so you can ease the pressure without losing your ambition. Many of the people who choose therapy for high achievers here are South Asian and BIPOC professionals carrying family expectations along with their own. With offices in West Los Angeles and Pasadena, plus secure telehealth across California, therapy for high achievers is within reach wherever you are.

Who this is for

You do not have to relate to all of these. If a few feel familiar, this work may be for you.

Imposter syndrome

You have the track record, yet a part of you is braced to be found out and discounts every win as luck.

People-pleasing

You say yes before checking what you have left, and struggle to set boundaries without guilt.

Burnout

Exhaustion that sleep does not fix, irritability, or numbness toward things you used to care about.

Perfectionism

The finish line keeps moving, and nothing ever feels quite good enough to rest on.

Self-worth tied to achievement

One mistake or a slow quarter can feel like proof you are not enough as a person.

The weight of expectations

The cultural and family pressure of being the one who was meant to make the sacrifice worth it.

How we help

We are a group practice, so we match you to the clinician whose style and lived understanding fit what you are carrying. Our approach is warm but direct, evidence-based, and culturally informed.

  • Modalities chosen for you, not a template: EMDR, Internal Family Systems (IFS), psychodynamic work, and Gottman or Emotionally Focused Therapy for couples.
  • Room for the whole story: career, identity, family, and the parts of you that only surface when it is safe to stop performing.
  • Care that respects South Asian and BIPOC experiences, with clinicians fluent in English, Punjabi, and Hindi.

As our practice puts it, the best therapy happens when you do not have to translate your world for your therapist.

Rates & logistics

  • Sessions: 50 minutes, weekly or as fits your schedule.
  • Locations: in-person in West Los Angeles and Pasadena, or secure telehealth anywhere in California.
  • Consultation: a free consultation to feel whether the fit is right before you commit.
  • Fees: our clinicians work across a range of rates depending on the therapist and format. We are happy to share indicative fees and discuss options on your consultation call.

Frequently asked questions

What is therapy for high achievers?

It is therapy tailored to accomplished, driven professionals whose success can mask anxiety, perfectionism, imposter syndrome, or self-worth tied to constant achievement. At Salonia Singh Therapy in California, it blends evidence-based methods with culturally informed care so you can address the pressure underneath the performance without losing your ambition.

Will therapy make me less ambitious?

No. The goal is not to reduce your drive but to change your relationship with it. Many people find that as perfectionism and the need to prove themselves ease, they keep their ambition while gaining more rest, clarity, and steadier self-worth.
